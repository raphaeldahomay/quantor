PV of a Future Single Cash Flow

With linear discounting:

\[ PV = \frac{CF}{(1+r*n)} \]


With standard discounting:

\[ PV = \frac{CF}{(1+r)^n} \]


With continuous discounting:

\[ PV = \frac{CF}{e^{r*n}} \]


FV of a Present Single Cash Flow

With linear discounting:

\[ FV = {CF}*{(1+r*n)} \]


With standard discounting:

\[ FV = {CF}*{(1+r)^n} \]


With continuous discounting:

\[ FV = {CF}*{e^{r*n}} \]


PV of Annuities

received at the start of the period:

\[ PV = CF \cdot \frac{1 - (1 + r)^{-n}}{r} \]


received at the end of the period:

\[ PV = CF \cdot \frac{1 - (1 + r)^{-n}}{r} \cdot (1 + r) \]


FV of Annuities

received at the start of the period:

\[ FV = CF \cdot \frac{(1 + r)^{n} - 1}{r} \]


received at the end of the period:

\[ FV = CF \cdot \frac{(1 + r)^{n} - 1}{r} \cdot (1 + r) \]


The Internal rate of an Investment generating equal CFs

\[ 0 = -CF_0 + \frac{CF_1}{(1 + IRR)} + \frac{CF_2}{(1 + IRR)^2} + \cdots + \frac{CF_n}{(1 + IRR)^n} \]


PV of an Annuity paid/received infinitely

\[ PV = \frac{CF}{r} \]

PV of an Annuity growing at a constant g rate and paid/received infinitely

\[ PV = \frac{CF}{(r - g)} \]
