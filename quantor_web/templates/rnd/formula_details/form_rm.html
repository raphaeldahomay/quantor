Finding the Variance

\[ \mathrm{Var}(X) = \frac{1}{n-1} \sum_{i=1}^{n} \left( x_i - \bar{x} \right)^2 \]

Finding the Semi-Variance

\[ \mathrm{SemiVar}(X) = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} \min\left( x_i - \bar{x},\, 0 \right)^2 \]

Finding the Std Dev or Downside Dev

\[ \sigma = \sqrt{\mathrm{Var}(X)} \]

\[ \mathrm{DownDev} = \sqrt{\mathrm{SemiVar}(X)} \]
