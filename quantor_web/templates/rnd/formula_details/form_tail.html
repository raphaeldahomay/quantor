Finding the Gaussian VaR or ES

Gaussian VaR:

\[ \text{VaR}_{c}^{\text{Gaussian}} = -\left( \mu_p + \sigma_p \Phi^{-1}(1-c) \right) \]

Gaussian ES:

\[ \text{ES}_{c}^{\text{Gaussian}} = -\left( \mu_p + \sigma_p \frac{\phi\!\left(\Phi^{-1}(1-c)\right)}{1-c} \right) \]

Finding the Historical VaR or ES:

Gaussian VaR:

\[ \text{VaR}_{c}^{\text{Hist}} = - R_{(\lceil n(1-c) \rceil)} \]

Gaussian ES:

\[ \text{ES}_{c}^{\text{Hist}} = - \frac{1}{\lceil n(1-c) \rceil} \sum_{i=1}^{\lceil n(1-c) \rceil} R_{(i)} \]

Finding the Cornish-Fisher VaR

\[ \text{VaR}_{c}^{\text{CF}} = - \sigma_p \left[ z + \frac{1}{6}(z^2 - 1)\gamma_1 + \frac{1}{24}(z^3 - 3z)\gamma_2 - \frac{1}{36}(2z^3 - 5z)\gamma_1^2 \right] \]
with:
\[ z = \Phi^{-1}(1-c) \]
