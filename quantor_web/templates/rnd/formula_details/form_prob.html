Finding the standardized z-score

\[ z = \frac{x-\mu}{\sigma} \]

Finding the standard normal CDF

\[ \Phi(z) \;=\; P(Z \le z) \;=\; \int_{-\infty}^{z} \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} \,e^{-t^{2}/2}\,dt \]

Finding the normal CDF

\[ P(X \le x) \;=\; \Phi\!\left(\frac{x-\mu}{\sigma}\right) \]

Finding the standard normal z-score

\[ \Phi^{-1}(p) \;=\; \inf\{\,z \in \mathbb{R} \;:\; \Phi(z) \ge p \,\} \]

Finding the normal z-score

\[ F_X^{-1}(p) \;=\; \inf\{\,x \in \mathbb{R} \;:\; F_X(x) \ge p \,\} \]

Finding the ES Coefficient for a certain confidence level

\[ c_{\mathrm{ES}}(\alpha) = \frac{\phi\!\left(\Phi^{-1}(\alpha)\right)}{1-\alpha} \]
