Finding the Skewness

\[ \text{Skewness} \;=\; \frac{1}{(n-1)\, \sigma^{3}} \sum_{i=1}^{n} (x_i - \bar{x})^{3} \]

Finding the Kurtosis

\[ \text{Excess Kurtosis} \;=\; \frac{1}{(n-1)\, \sigma^{4}} \sum_{i=1}^{n} (x_i - \bar{x})^{4} \;-\; 3 \]
