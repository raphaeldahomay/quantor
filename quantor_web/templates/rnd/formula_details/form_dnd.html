Finding the Covariance

\[ \operatorname{Cov}(X,Y) = \frac{1}{n-1} \sum_{i=1}^{n} \left(X_i - \bar{X}\right) \left(Y_i - \bar{Y}\right) \]

Finding the Correlation

\[ \rho_{X,Y} = \frac{\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)} {\sigma_X \sigma_Y} \]

Finding the Covariance Matrix (for n assets)

\[ \Sigma = \begin{pmatrix} \sigma_1^2 & \operatorname{Cov}(1,2) & \cdots & \operatorname{Cov}(1,n) \\ \operatorname{Cov}(2,1) & \sigma_2^2 & \cdots & \operatorname{Cov}(2,n) \\ \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\ \operatorname{Cov}(n,1) & \operatorname{Cov}(n,2) & \cdots & \sigma_n^2 \end{pmatrix} \]

Finding the Variance of a portfolio

(Matrix form)

\[ \sigma_p^2 = \mathbf{w}^\top \Sigma \mathbf{w} \]

(Standard form)

\[ \sigma_p^2 = \sum_{i=1}^{n} \sum_{j=1}^{n} w_i w_j \operatorname{Cov}(i,j) \]

Finding the Variance of a 2-asset portfolio

\[ \sigma_p^2 = w_1^2 \sigma_1^2 + w_2^2 \sigma_2^2 + 2 w_1 w_2 \operatorname{Cov}(1,2) \]

Finding the Diversification effect

\[ \text{Diversification Effect} = \left( \sum_{i=1}^{n} w_i \sigma_i \right) - \sigma_p \]
